Sales Forecasting Dashboard

Advanced time-series forecasting and interactive sales trends dashboard.

Business Problem

Retailers and businesses struggle to anticipate product demand, leading to stockouts of popular items or bloated inventories of low-demand categories. This mismatch leads to lost revenue, elevated warehousing costs, and operational inefficiencies.

Solution

Developed a time-series forecasting engine utilizing Python modeling libraries, displaying projections and historical trends within a Power BI dashboard. This framework helps supply chain planners evaluate weekly and monthly sales projections to align purchase orders.

Methodology

1. Data Processing & Stationarity Testing: Extracted historical sales volumes, aggregated timelines, and tested stationarity using the Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) test to apply log differentials where required.

2. Model Selection & Fitting: Built and compared statsmodels ARIMA, seasonal SARIMAX, and Prophet. Selected the best estimator based on minimizing AIC/BIC values and cross-validation errors.

3. Inference Pipeline: Set up a script to generate 12-month future forecasts, outputting expected values alongside 95% confidence intervals to represent forecasting uncertainties.

4. Power BI Dashboard Integration: Fed model outputs into Power BI, designing product category filters, historical trends comparisons, and forecast interval overlays.

Key Results

  • ✔ Achieved a Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) of under 8.5% across primary product classes.
  • ✔ Enabled demand planners to optimize inventory, proposing a reduction in overstock by up to 18%.
  • ✔ Delivered interactive dashboards that dynamically refresh, replacing manual spreadsheet macros.
