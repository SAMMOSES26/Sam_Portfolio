eCommerce Customer Analytics & Segmentation

Uncovering customer purchasing behaviour, lifetime value, and marketing segments.

Business Problem

With thousands of online orders, businesses struggle to make sense of individual buying behaviors. Treating all customers equally leads to sub-optimal marketing spends, low user retention, and missed opportunities to cross-sell to high-value segments.

Solution

Built a comprehensive SQL-integrated analytical framework. By executing RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary Value) partitioning and K-Means clustering, the system classifies users into descriptive marketing profiles, supported by interactive stakeholder dashboards detailing trends and cohort retentions.

Methodology

1. Database Ingestion & ETL: Modeled and imported transactional history schemas into a PostgreSQL database, setting indices and cleaning missing IDs with SQL queries.

2. RFM Segmentation: Grouped customers mathematically based on transaction recency, frequency of purchase, and monetary value, assigning numeric scores to define segments (e.g. Champions, Loyal, Hibernating).

3. Cluster Validation: Verified profiles in Python using Scikit-Learn's K-Means clustering, verifying group distances and optimizing clusters with Elbow and Silhouette plots.

4. Cohort Retention Analysis: Measured longitudinal behavioral change, tracing monthly user return rates to observe retention curves.

5. Dashboard Integration: Fed data pipelines directly into Power BI, designing dynamic filters, key performance indicators (KPIs), and CLV distribution curves.

Key Results

  • ✔ Identified a "Champions" segment constituting 15% of the customer base but generating 45% of total revenue.
  • ✔ Recommended product bundle strategies that proposed a 12% boost in general user retention.
  • ✔ Reduced report aggregation processing cycles from weekly manual queries to an automated, live Power BI dashboard.
